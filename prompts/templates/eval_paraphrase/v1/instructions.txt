# paraphrase-instructions v1
You are helping build a test set for a question-answering system over company annual reports
(SEC 10-K and 20-F filings). Below this block is a batch of questions, one JSON object per line:
{"case_id": "...", "personas": ["...", ...], "question": "..."}

For every question, write one paraphrase for each persona listed in its "personas" field: the
same question, worded the way that kind of person would really type it to a research assistant
or a chat tool. Make the wording genuinely different from the original (word order, vocabulary,
sentence shape, abbreviations), not a light edit, and keep it something a real person would send.

PERSONAS
- sell_side_shorthand: a sell-side analyst writing a quick note: tickers, FY24-style periods,
  street shorthand ("top line", "opex", "GM", "y/y", "vs"), no full sentences.
- terse_pm: a busy portfolio manager: very short, clipped, a few words, often no verb.
- conversational_retail: a retail investor chatting: casual full sentences, plain words, may
  open with "hey" or "so", may use the company's everyday name.
- typo_prone: someone typing fast on a phone: a few typos, missing capitals or punctuation,
  "pls" / "thx" style abbreviations.
- buried_ask: the question sits inside one or two sentences of context about why the person
  is asking (a model update, a meeting, a client call), with the actual ask at the end.

MEANING RULES (most important: a paraphrase that changes the meaning is useless)
1. Keep the company. Name it with its ticker, its short name or its legal name; never swap
   in a different company, a parent, a subsidiary or a competitor.
2. Keep the period exactly. These are fiscal years: write them as "FY24", "FY 2024",
   "fiscal 2024", "fiscal year 2024" or "the fiscal year ended <date>" when the date is in
   the question. Never turn a fiscal period into a calendar one: do not write "calendar",
   "CY24", "2024 calendar year", and do not write a bare "in 2024" when the question covers
   several companies. Never add or change a quarter, "TTM", "LTM", "YTD", "trailing",
   "last year", "this year", "latest" or "most recent". If the question names two years,
   keep both.
3. Keep the metric. Use its name or a common synonym or analyst shorthand for the very same
   figure ("revenue", "net sales", "top line"); never swap it for a nearby figure (revenue is
   not profit, operating income is not net income, gross margin is not gross profit).
4. Keep the operation: a lookup stays a lookup, a growth rate stays a growth rate, a
   difference stays a difference, a ratio stays the same ratio in the same direction, a
   "top 3" stays a top 3 (same number, same direction: highest stays highest).
5. Keep the scope: for a question about a group of companies, name the same group in the same
   words or a clear equivalent; never widen or narrow the group (do not turn "big tech" into
   "the S&P 100", "all companies" or "tech stocks"). Do not add a segment, region or product
   the question does not name, and do not drop one it does name.
6. Typos (typo_prone persona) go only in ordinary words. Never misspell the company, the
   metric or the period: write them exactly as in a correct form.
7. Do not answer the question, add facts or numbers, or ask for anything extra.

REPLY FORMAT (exact)
Reply with one JSON object per line, one line per requested persona of every question, and
nothing else: no introduction, no numbering, no commentary, no blank lines between lines. Each
object has exactly the keys "case_id", "persona" and "paraphrase":
{"case_id": "<the case_id, copied exactly>", "persona": "<one requested persona id>", "paraphrase": "<your paraphrase>"}
Copy each case_id and persona id exactly as given. Use standard JSON (double quotes; escape a
double quote inside the paraphrase as \"). Keep each paraphrase on one line. Cover every
question in the batch, in the order given, before you stop.
