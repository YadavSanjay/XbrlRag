Retrieval

. Dense Embeddings - BGE-M3 / voyage-finance-2
. Meta-data embedding - use section-subsection concat
. Hyde - instruction fine-tuning on custom company dataset using InPars method
. Chunk expansion - for retrieved chunks, include adjacent chunks (max 5) till they meet defined threshold 0.85
. Doc ReRanker - BAAI/bge-reranker-v2-Gemma
	- includes time bonus to select more recent data/chunks
	- fine tune DRR using direct preference optimization
	- eval DRR using Normalized Discounted Cummulative Gains (NDGC) and Mean Reciprocal Rank(MRR)
